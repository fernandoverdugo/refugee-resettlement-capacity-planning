Refugee Resettlement Capacity Planner

Explore hypothetical service-demand scenarios, staffing capacity, budget constraints, and equitable resource allocation.

This tool is for service-resource planning. It does not recommend refugee placement or make individual-level decisions. Staffing and cost inputs are hypothetical unless replaced with validated operational data.
How to use this planner

Start with the loaded defaults, then change one or two assumptions. Sliders are for quick scenario exploration; the number boxes let you enter exact values.

1. Set expected demandUse the reference monthly demand as the starting point. Lower and higher multipliers create alternative demand scenarios.
2. Set available capacityAdjust staffing, caseload, utilization, and budget. The live capacity estimate updates before you run the optimizer.
3. Compare scenariosRun the planner and compare coverage, unmet demand, staffing use, cost, and binding constraints.
Quick glossary
FTE
Full-time equivalent. One FTE represents the workload capacity of one full-time staff position.
Horizon
How many future months the planner evaluates.
Demand
The number of cases assumed to require service during the planning period.
Coverage
The share of demand that available resources can serve. 100% means all modeled demand can be served.
Utilization
The share of theoretical staff capacity assumed to be usable. For example, 0.85 means 85%.
Binding constraint
A limit that is fully used and prevents the model from serving more demand, such as the staffing budget or total FTE available.
